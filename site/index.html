What it does

One tool, from link to library

Everything runs locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Download

Paste a link and Sieve analyzes it and picks the right formats for you.

  • 1000+ sites — YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Twitch and more, via the yt-dlp engine.
  • 8 one-tap presets for the common "just get me the video / audio" cases.
  • Reliable background queue with pause, resume, and retry that survives being killed.

Transcode

Re-encode your own media on-device with real, tunable controls.

  • 52 presets across H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, ProRes, DNxHR, audio-only and more.
  • Hardware-accelerated H.264/HEVC via Android MediaCodec, with automatic software fallback.
  • Software x264 x265 AV1 via a self-built, full-GPL FFmpeg.

Library

Grant one output folder and your files land straight there.

  • Pick a folder once through the Storage Access Framework; downloads and transcodes write to it.
  • Built-in browser over your granted folder.
  • Subtitle export and frame extraction straight into your library.

Private & free

Yours to keep, with nothing watching over your shoulder.

  • Nothing is uploaded — all downloading and transcoding happen on your device.
  • Self-updating engine — the downloader updates itself so newly-broken sites get fixed fast, no reinstall.
  • Fully open-source under GPLv3. No ads, no tracking, no account.
How it works

Three taps from link to file

No setup, no sign-in. Open Sieve and go.

01

Paste a link

Drop in a URL from any supported site. Sieve analyzes it and finds the available formats.

02

Pick a preset

Choose from eight one-tap presets — best video, a specific resolution, or audio-only.

03

Download

It runs in the background queue and lands in your chosen folder — ready to transcode if you like.

License & source

Open source, and openly compliant

Sieve for Android is licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 (GPLv3), because it links GPL-licensed software at runtime — a self-built, full-GPL build of FFmpeg and youtubedl-android (which bundles yt-dlp). Because those components are covered by the GPL, the combined work as a whole is licensed under GPLv3. Every piece of source is linked below.

FFmpeg — corresponding source offer

Sieve bundles a self-built FFmpeg (executed as a child process, never linked in-process), statically linked with x264 and x265 and built full-GPL. The complete corresponding source is the exact upstream commit of each project below, plus the build recipe transcode/build-ffmpeg/ffbuild.sh in the app repository. All three are unmodified upstream checkouts — no local patches.
